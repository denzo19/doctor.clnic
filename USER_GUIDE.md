# Clinics Management App User Guide

## Overview

Clinics Management App helps clinic staff manage patients, appointments, check-ins, visits, prescriptions, lab orders, lab results, vital signs, employees, users, permissions, and clinic schedules. Menus are role-based, so each user only sees the options assigned to their group.

## Getting Started

1. Open the app and sign in with your username and password.
2. Use the language switch in the top navigation to choose English or Arabic.
3. Use the top menu to open the pages available to your role.
4. Use the user menu area to log out or change your password when needed.

## Common Workflows

### Patients

- Open **Patients > Manage Patients** to search, add, edit, stop, or export patient records.
- Open a patient record to view demographics, vital signs, diseases, medications, lab results, and visit reports.
- Use **Unchecked Patient** to search patient history for patients who are not currently checked in.

### Check-In

- Use **Appointments Query & Check-in** to find booked appointments for the selected date and check in arriving patients.
- Use **Check-in Walk-in** to check in a patient without an appointment.
- Use **Check-in Lists** to view today's check-in lists by doctor.
- Doctors use **Checked-in Patients** to open today's checked-in patients, start visits, prescribe medications, or order lab tests.

### Visits

- From a checked-in patient, open the patient visit.
- Record diagnosis, symptoms, notes, medications, lab orders, and follow-up details.
- Save the visit when the consultation is complete.
- Existing visit reports can be viewed from the patient details page.

### Appointments

- Use **Manage Appointment** to review, edit, rebook, cancel, or export patient appointments.
- Use **Search & Cancel Appointment** to find and cancel booked appointments.
- Use **Doctor Appointments** to view a doctor's appointment schedule.

### Prescriptions

- Use **Manage Prescriptions** to create, view, print, update, delete draft prescriptions, or stop active prescriptions.
- Draft prescriptions can be edited or deleted.
- Finalized prescriptions are locked from editing.

### Lab And Vital Signs

- Use **Vital Signs** to add or edit patient vital sign records.
- Use **Lab Tests** to manage available laboratory tests.
- Use **Lab Orders** to create, view, print, edit, delete draft orders, or cancel ordered lab requests.
- Use **Enter Lab Results** to enter results for ordered lab tests and final-save completed orders.
- Completed lab results appear in the patient details page.

## Administration

Admin users can configure the system from the admin and clinic management menus:

- **Manage Users**, **Manage Groups**, and **Manage Permissions** control access.
- **Manage Menu Options** controls which menu items exist and how they are organized.
- **Manage Employees** stores clinic staff records and links employees to user accounts.
- **Manage Medical Centers**, **Doctor-Center Assignments**, **Doctor Weekly Programs**, and **Doctor Absences** define clinic availability.
- **Initiate Schedule** and **Extend Schedule** create appointment slots.
- **Doctor Speciality**, **ID Card**, **Diagnosis ICD Setup**, and **Diagnosis ICD Fine Tuning** support clinical setup.

## Tips

- Use search fields and filters before exporting reports.
- Inactive or stopped records usually remain visible for history but cannot be used for new work.
- Confirmation prompts appear before destructive actions such as delete, stop, or cancel.
- If a menu item is missing, ask an administrator to check your group permissions.
